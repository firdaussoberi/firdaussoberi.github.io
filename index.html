Firdaus Soberi

PhD Candidate in Particle Physics @ University of Edinburgh; ATLAS Collaboration.

I am a PhD candidate in Particle Physics at the University of Edinburgh and a member of the ATLAS Collaboration. My research focuses on Higgs-boson physics, particularly precision measurements and CP-violating interactions in the $H\rightarrow\gamma\gamma$ channel.

I also work on detector simulation and machine learning within ATLAS, with interests spanning generative modelling, scientific computing, and efficient software for large-scale physics analyses.

Research Interests

  • Higgs Physics & Effective Field Theory
  • Machine Learning & Generative Models
  • Detector Simulation & Reconstruction
  • Scientific Computing
  • High-Energy Physics
